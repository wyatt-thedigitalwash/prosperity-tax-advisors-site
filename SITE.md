# SITE.md

## Project

Prosperity Tax Advisors — full website rebuild.

A Tampa-based tax strategy and wealth advisory firm serving business owners, entrepreneurs, and 1099 high-income earners paying $100,000 or more annually in taxes. The firm operates at the intersection of tax planning, accounting, and wealth/CFO advisory. This is a rebuild of the existing WordPress site at prosperityta.com. New design, new structure, new copy. Native contact form integrated with GoHighLevel via webhook. Existing GHL booking widget retained for scheduling.

## Positioning

Strategic tax and wealth advisory for business owners whose finances have outgrown their CPA.

The firm is not a filing shop and not a direct-response tax-savings marketer. It is a credentialed advisory firm for serious operators. The tone of the site reflects that: quiet, specific, literary, confident. The case studies do the selling. The team's credentials do the credibility lift. Nothing shouts.

## Audience

Primary: Business owners and entrepreneurs with tax liability of $100,000 or more annually.
Secondary: 1099 high-income earners (physicians, consultants, real estate professionals) with similar tax exposure.
Tertiary: Seven-figure operators considering exit planning or multi-entity restructuring.

## Visual Direction

Direction: Private bank / boutique law firm.

Reference points:
- Brown Advisory (brownadvisory.com) — closest analog, private wealth firm tone
- Hirtle Callaghan (hirtlecallaghan.com) — editorial typography, muted color
- Cleary Gottlieb (clearygottlieb.com) — white-shoe law firm, case study depth
- Monocle Magazine — typographic philosophy, restrained editorial

Shared DNA: serifs do the heavy lifting, color is muted and purposeful, photography is real or absent, nothing shouts.

## Color Palette

Seven colors. Three (Plum, Aubergine, Teal) are drawn from the existing brand. Four (Ink, Cream, Bone, Graphite) are the editorial layer that makes the system feel like a private wealth firm rather than a fintech.

- Plum: #362E76 (primary dark, headlines, primary surfaces, CTA backgrounds)
- Aubergine: #693E82 (accent, pull quotes, case study numerals, highlights)
- Ink: #0D0F2A (body text, deepest surfaces)
- Teal: #09B5B7 (interactive accent only: link hover, focus rings, active nav state, small editorial details. Never a primary surface or CTA fill.)
- Cream: #FAF7F0 (page background)
- Bone: #E8E1D3 (secondary surface, section breaks)
- Graphite: #5C5A55 (captions, metadata, secondary text)

Pure black (#000) and pure white (#FFF) never appear. All borders, dividers, and muted text pull from this palette only.

## Typography

- Display / Headlines: Source Serif 4 (Google Fonts). Weights 400, 500, 600, 700. Variable optical sizing where supported.
- Body / UI: Inter (Google Fonts). Weights 400, 500, 600.

Rules:
- Headlines always sentence case. Never title case. Never all caps.
- Body copy 16-18px, line-height 1.6 to 1.75.
- Display sizes go large (72px on desktop H1) with tight tracking.
- No bold body copy. Emphasis via weight change in headlines only.
- Numbers in case studies get their own treatment: large Source Serif 4 at 1.0 line-height, almost architectural.

## Tone of Voice

Do:
- Write as if the reader is already serious
- State facts without exclamation
- Use specific numbers and named strategies
- Let the work speak
- Write in complete sentences that read as prose

Do not:
- Use words like "slash", "aggressive", "unlock", "discover", "secrets"
- Use urgency language ("Don't be one of them", "Stop overpaying")
- Use stat counters as design elements
- Start sentences with "Ready to..."
- Use em dashes anywhere
- Use phrases like "Book Your Intro Call Now"

## Design Anti-Patterns (Do Not Use)

- Stat counter animations
- Three-column service card grids
- Icon-in-circle feature blocks
- "Why Choose Us" sections
- Stock photography
- Testimonial carousels
- Gradient backgrounds or buttons
- Pill buttons with border-radius greater than 4px
- Lucide icons used decoratively (only functional use allowed: menu, close, external link)
- Centered hero with CTA and feature grid below

## Services

Four service lines. Each is treated with full depth on the Services page, not reduced to a card grid.

### Tax Advisory
Year-round tax strategy for business owners and high-income earners. Identification, substantiation, and activation of strategies drawn from over 1,500 IRS-approved options. Entity structure optimization, retirement plan design, timing strategies, and risk-managed tax efficiency. Not preparation. Not filing. Strategy.

### Client Advisory Services
Monthly reconciliations, accurate financial statements, and audit insurance. Prosperity finds errors in 80 to 90 percent of new tax clients' books. Specialized expertise in health, mental health, and group practices. Includes fraud detection, process review, and support for exit planning or business sale.

### Start-Up Business Services
Foundational accounting and tax setup for new entrepreneurs. Entity selection, bookkeeping systems, compliance infrastructure, and tax strategy built in from day one.

### Fractional CFO Services
Forecasting, budgeting, team compensation strategy, and long-term financial planning for seven-figure operators. Exit planning and growth-stage strategic support. The insight of a CFO without the overhead of hiring one full-time.

## The Prosperity Process

Single unified five-step process. Replaces the two overlapping "process" sections on the current site.

1. Clarity Audit — Full review of financial reality. Identifies inefficiencies, missed deductions, and structural opportunities.
2. Custom Wealth Tax Blueprint — Personalized strategy aligned with income, goals, and business structure. Strategies curated from over 1,500 IRS-approved options.
3. Entity Engineering — Legal structure optimization to reduce taxable income and protect assets.
4. Empowerment Through Education — Clients are taught the reasoning behind every strategy, so they remain in control.
5. Execution and Oversight — Full implementation, quarterly reviews, and audit-ready documentation.
